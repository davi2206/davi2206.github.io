## Birthday messages matter
Some people make a big fuzz about their birthdays, invite everyone for a big party, cake, karaoke, drinks, dinner, lights, balloons, and much more. Others prefer to let the day pass in silence, with little to no celebration. No gifts, no guests, no grand plans. <br>
Weather people are in the first or the second group, most people like getting messages on their birthdays. Not because they necessarily like the attention, but because a personal message says "I remember you". Peoples birthdays are a great time to let them know that you still think of them, and they still mean something to you, even if it is just the faint memory of a friendship. <br>
It doesn't have to be a long profound message. Even a few words, can put a smile on someone’s face. The important part is, it should be somewhat personal. The automatic Facebook message you can send with one click seems a little hollow, at least to me. 

{% box %} Happy Birthday! Hope your day is filled with happiness and laughter! {% endbox %}

One thing that is important to remember is that the thought is not quite enough, in this case. You can have all the intentions in the world, but if you don't get around to sending the message, it doesn't matter. Sending a short message to someone means infinitely more then planing to send an essay or a "perfect message", that you never get around to.

Sometimes, you want to write someone, and you just can't find the words. Believe me, I have spent too many hours staring at empty chat windows, and never written anything, because I didn't know how. But in reality it doesn't have to be so hard. We just have to accept, that sometimes the perfect message, is simply the one you send.
